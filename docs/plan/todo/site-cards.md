# Jack's Space — Every page in the home page's card style

Your ask of 6 Oct 2026, after the home page became colour cards (mockup E): "我要你帮我全部都改成这个风格。当然，content先不改。" Every page takes on the home page's look; what the pages say and do stays as it is.

Carried over from the home page's plan, still open: whether its toy should only rotate through the effects that fill the frame on their own; some, such as Mouse Trail, are one dot until the mouse moves.

---

## Decisions and tasks

My proposal of 6 Oct 2026, for your OK.

- **D1. One look everywhere, the same content.** What changes is how pages look (cards, colours, corners), not their words, lists or features.
- **D2. Each part of the site wears its menu colour:** Writing pink, Lab green, Collection orange, Travel blue, About purple. Now keeps the Now card's purple, the Changelog takes amber, Random the Lab's green, and the 404 page a plain card.
- **D3. Every main page's top becomes a colour card:** the big title and its lead in white on the part's colours, with its tabs, switch or buttons under it. It's one shared piece (`PageTop`), so all ten pages that use it change together. A post's top becomes a card in its topic's colour.
- **D4. Lists become cards of the same family:** Writing's posts (each with its topic's colour), the Lab's items, the Changelog's days, About's parts and Now's items; plain cards where there's no colour to show, the part's colour where there is.
- **D5. Calm where it has to be:** a post's text, a tool's workspace, a game, an effect and a world keep their working area plain; only what frames them changes.
- **D6. The ground stays the site's own** dark blue-black and light theme, as on the new home page.

Tasks, one at a time; one commit and push at the end of each once you've looked:

- **S1. The shared parts and the simple pages:** each part's colours as tokens; `PageTop` as a colour card; the cards' corners; your links as coloured tiles everywhere they appear (About, a post's profile card), from one piece; "Check these too!" as cards. Pages: Writing, the tag pages, About, Now, the Changelog, Random and 404.
- **S2. Posts:** the top in the topic's colour; the profile card and Site info as cards.
- **S3. The Lab:** the Lab home's sections and cards; the tools', games', effects' and worlds' headers and frames.
- **S4. Collection and Travel:** their tops; the tabs and the Globe | Flat switch as pills. The turntable and the map stay as they are.
- **S5. Each group's finish:** changelog line, TESTING.md, the sweep, commit and push.
