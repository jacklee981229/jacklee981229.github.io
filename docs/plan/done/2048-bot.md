# Jack's Space — 2048 Bot

A **Bot** button on Jack's 2048: click it and the game plays itself.

Gathered in chat on 4–5 Oct 2026; the decisions and tasks are below.

## What I picked

- **Where:** a Bot button beside Undo and New game. It reads Stop while the bot plays.
- **Leaderboard:** a game the bot played in never goes on it.
- **Best:** bot games don't change it.
- **At 2048:** the bot stops at "You made 2048!"; I choose Keep going, and click Bot again if I want more.

## Not in this plan

- A speed control.
- A hint button (one suggested move).
- A bot for the other games.

---

## Decisions and tasks

One task at a time, as before.

- **D64. The bot looks ahead** (expectimax search). For each direction it plays the move with `move()` from `src/games/2048/rules.js`, so the rules stay in one place. It then weighs every cell where a new 2 (nine times in ten) or 4 could appear, and looks one more move ahead (two when the board is nearly full). A board scores well for empty cells, the biggest tile in a corner, rows and columns that rise steadily towards that corner, and equal tiles side by side. The bot plays the direction with the best average. It's `src/games/2048/bot.js`: plain JavaScript in the browser, a few milliseconds a move, no packages. It usually reaches 2048, not every game; B1 measures how often.
- **D65. It plays through the same step as the keys**, about five moves a second, so slides, the score, the clock and Undo behave as always. The Bot button sits beside Undo and New game and reads Stop while it plays.
- **D66. It stops** on Stop; on a move key, Z or a swipe (you take over); on New game; at "You made 2048!" (you choose Keep going) or "No more moves"; and when the tab is left.
- **D67. A game the bot played in is marked**, and the mark is saved with the game, so a reload keeps it; Undo doesn't clear it, a new game does. A marked game never goes on the leaderboard (its end messages show without the name box) and doesn't change Best. The mark is my addition: it's what makes your two choices work.

- **D74. 2048's buttons are icons where room is short.** With Bot added, the scores and the three buttons no longer fitted one line above a laptop-sized board, which pushes the board's foot off the screen. Each button is now an icon and its name; where the row would wrap (every board from a small phone's up to the biggest, 704 px), the names hide and the buttons are round icons, with the name kept as the tooltip and for screen readers. On a phone the buttons have a line of their own, so the names show. This is my change to your pick of words on the buttons.

Tasks:

- **B1. The bot's brain:** `bot.js` and its unit tests. *Done when:* the tests show it always picks a direction that moves, picks none on a finished board, and takes an obvious join; and a 50-game seeded run gives the win rate and the time per move for the report.
  *Result (5 Oct 2026):* 34 of 50 seeded games reached 2048 (68%); the rest ended at 1024 (12) or 512 (4). About 5 ms a move on this PC, 84 ms at the slowest. Good enough: a perfect record isn't the aim.
- **B2. The button:** Bot and Stop in `game.js`, the mark, and the stops. *Done when:* at 1280 px the bot plays, Stop stops it, an arrow key takes over, it stops at both messages, a bot game reaches neither the leaderboard nor Best; and you've tried it.
- **B3. Finish:** a changelog line, a TESTING.md section and the 2048 line in CLAUDE.md's "Where things live"; then your test, the wide checks (the extra button must not wrap the top row on narrow screens), commit and push.
