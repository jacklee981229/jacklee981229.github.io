# Jack's Space — Little Worlds: Jack's Town

An endless traffic town that a visitor can just stare at. Cars, roads, junctions, traffic lights and the other usual
parts, running by themselves forever.

It isn't a canned animation, it's a running program: like a game that a bot plays by itself, without being a game.
Every car decides each moment for itself (keep a gap, brake for red, turn, give way), and every junction's light
changes, so the town never loops.

Gathered in chat on 4–5 Oct 2026; the decisions and tasks are below.

## What I picked

- **Where:** a new Lab section, **Little Worlds** (things that run by themselves). The first one is **Jack's Town**.
- **Look:** top-down and flat.
- **Endless:** one town that fits the window; it's endless because the trips never stop.
- **Reference:** the calm, minimal road-building games: their flat, soft look, and homes and places in matching
  colours, so every car drives somewhere for a reason. Refer to them, don't copy them: build our own version that
  matches the website. Don't name those games anywhere in the repo.
- **Extras for later**, each its own task: night headlights in the dark theme; smart lights and rush hour.

## Not in this plan

- A town that grows and a bot that lays new roads (kept as an idea).
- Buses, people crossing, roundabouts.
- Visitor touches: following a car, tapping a light, live counters.

## Add-on: thought bubbles (5 Oct 2026)

Now and then a moving car shows a thinking cloud, the kind a person thinks in, in a comic: what's on its mind is
where it's going; stuck in a jam, it's angry.

- **Thought:** a tiny copy of the building it's heading to. Going home, a little house; leaving town, a suitcase.
- **How often:** a few at a time.
- **Jam:** the comic anger mark.
- **Animation:** the classic pop: the small circle, the middle one, then the cloud.

## Decisions and tasks

Your plan of 5 Oct 2026, above: an endless traffic town that runs by itself, for visitors to stare at. One task at a time, as before.

- **D58. A new Lab section, "Little Worlds"**, for things that run by themselves. The first is **Jack's Town** at `/lab/world/town/`, laid out like an Effects page: the Lab header, the town, then "Check these too!".
- **D59. Top-down and flat, one town that fits the window**, endless because the trips never stop. The calm road-building games are a reference only; the look is our own: the site's theme colours, the topic colours for homes, places and cars, soft rounded roads. No game is named anywhere in the repo.
- **D60. Every trip has a reason:** homes and places come in matching colours (three colours); a car leaves its home, drives to a place of its colour, waits a moment, and drives home.
- **D61. A real simulation, not an animation.** The roads, lights and driving rules are in `src/lib/town/`, run in fixed, seeded steps, so the tests can replay a town exactly. Canvas, written from scratch, no packages. Cars drive on the left; the lights run on a timer for now.
- **D62. It never jams for good:** a car enters a junction only when there's room beyond it, and a car stuck for over a minute fades out (a new trip starts later).
- **D63. The Effects' manners:** theme colours, Pause and Play, a still picture under reduced motion, no work while off screen, and its script loads only on its own page. The Effects stage (`src/effects/stage.js`) is reused if it fits; if it doesn't, I report before building another.
- **D86. Thought bubbles** (the add-on above): now and then a car that's driving thinks, in a comic thought cloud above it with two small circles trailing down to it. Inside is a tiny copy of where it's heading: the place in its colour and shape, ring and all; a little house in its home's colour when it's going home; a suitcase when it's leaving town. Parked cars and cars backing out don't think.
- **D87. A few at a time:** at most one bubble for every eight homes (at least one, at most four), each for about three seconds. A car thinks at most once a trip, at a random moment while it drives, and never where its bubble would touch another.
- **D88. Stuck in a jam:** a driving car that has stood still longer than the longest red light (14 seconds now, so 15) shows the comic anger mark in the stop lights' red until it moves on. Angry bubbles don't count towards D87's limit: up to three of them at once.
- **D89. How bubbles look and run:** the classic pop (the small circle by the car, then the middle one, then the cloud puffs out) and a fade at the end, timed by the town's own clock, so Pause stops them too. The cloud is the page's colour with a thin outline and a soft shadow, so it reads in both themes, and it moves below or to the left of its car near the picture's edge. Who thinks what, and when, is `src/lib/town/thoughts.js` with a seed of its own, so the traffic plays out exactly as before; `src/worlds/town.js` draws the bubbles over the cars.

- **D90. Bye-bye, and longer thoughts** (your word, 5 Oct 2026; this changes D86 and D87). A car leaving town thinks of a hand waving bye-bye, in the lights' amber, waving from the wrist, instead of a suitcase. Every thought stays five seconds instead of three, so a car only starts one with 2.4 blocks or more of its trip still ahead.
- **D91. Click a car to see where it's going** (your word, 5 Oct 2026). A click or a tap on a car that's going somewhere (driving, backing out, or about to) pops up its thought at once, whatever the limit, and the bubbles too near it give way. A car parked with nowhere to go yet has nothing to think of, so a click on it does nothing. Over a car that would think, the pointer is a hand. The line under the title says "Click a car to see where it's going, or add and remove cars."
- **D92. Add car and Remove car** (your word, 5 Oct 2026). Two buttons beside Pause. Add car brings one more car in along a road from outside town, to live in an empty home if there is one (one whose car has left town for good), or else to stay, driving from place to place in its colour; up to half as many cars again as there are homes (eleven more in a town of 22 homes). Remove car sends a parked car, picked at random, out of town for good, and pops up its bye-bye; a resident's home stays empty until Add car brings someone to live there. A button greys out while it can't.
- **D93. Detours, so queues can't lock up** (found while testing D92). A car that has waited 20 seconds at a junction (longer than any red light) with its way on still blocked takes another way across that's free, and finds its way from there; it's the same trip, so it doesn't think twice. Before this, a queue could reach back across junctions until a ring of streets blocked each other, and a car stuck a minute was taken off in sight (D62). That already happened in the live town on screens 1,440 px wide and more (towns of 9 by 5 blocks and up: in 12 hours, 1 to 4 towns in 5 jammed, up to 113 cars taken off), and Add car made it happen in smaller towns too.
- **D96. Lights that answer the traffic** (your question of 5 Oct 2026, why cars wait at a green-for-nobody; this changes D61, where the lights ran on a timer). A junction's light stays green for one road while nobody waits on the other. Once someone does, it goes over after at least 4 seconds of green: at once if nobody's coming on its own road within half a block, after 9 if they keep coming. Then 2 seconds of amber and 1.5 of red for everyone, as before. A car waits at most as long as before (14 seconds), but mostly far less: in four towns over two hours, the time a car stands at junctions halved (15.0 to 7.7 seconds a trip), and stops over 8 seconds went from 37% of stops to 7%.
- **D98. Gentler starts and stops** (your word, 5 Oct 2026). A car takes about two seconds to get up to speed (it took under one) and about a second to slow to a stop from full speed (it took under half a second), so it starts to slow sooner before a red, a queue or a turn.
